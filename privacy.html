Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

LuxeClip is made by The Luxe Collective. In short: we don't collect any data. Everything you save stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

What LuxeClip stores

LuxeClip saves the things you copy — text, links, images and files you choose to keep — along with the time and the app or device they came from. This is stored on your device.

Sync through your iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your clips sync between your iPhone and Mac through Apple's iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The Luxe Collective can't see or access this data. If you create or join a shared Team pinboard, only the clips you add to it are shared, and only with the people invited to it.

The keyboard

The LuxeClip keyboard asks for Full Access so it can read the clipboard and show your saved clips. It does not record what you type, and it doesn't send keystrokes or clipboard contents anywhere except your own iCloud sync.

Text in images

LuxeClip reads text in images on your device, using Apple's built-in text recognition, so you can search screenshots. Images aren't sent to any server for this.

What we don't do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of data. LuxeClip contains no third-party tracking code.

Deleting your data

Delete any clip in the app, or use Setup → Clear History. Deleting the app removes its data from your device; you can remove synced data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at luxetn.com/contact.
