Support

LuxeClip keeps everything you copy on your iPhone and Mac, so you can find and paste it again — text, links, addresses, screenshots and templates with blanks you fill in.

Getting started on iPhone

Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → LuxeClip, then tap LuxeClip and turn on Allow Full Access. In any app, hold the globe key and choose LuxeClip. Whatever you copied is saved the moment the keyboard opens; tap a clip to type it.

Why Full Access?

The keyboard needs Full Access to read your clipboard and show your saved clips, which live in storage shared with the LuxeClip app. LuxeClip never records what you type and never sends anything to us.

Sync

Clips sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. If clips aren't syncing, make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device, then open LuxeClip → Setup → Sync Now.
